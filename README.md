Pomento TextClean Privacy Policy
Effective date: 18 October 2026
Version: 1.0.0
Developer: Dr. Dinesh Kumar Jangra
Brand: Pomento.in

1. What the extension does
Pomento TextClean is a Chrome extension for cleaning copied text. Users can paste text into its popup or choose “Use page selection” to import text they have highlighted on a supported webpage. It reformats the text locally, including removing common spacing artefacts and optionally joining PDF line wraps.

2. Text handled and how it is used
The extension processes text you paste and, only when you request it, the highlighted text selected on the active webpage. Such text may contain personal or sensitive information depending on what you choose to enter or select. The extension uses this text only to produce a cleaned version for you.

The selected text is retrieved using Chrome’s activeTab and scripting permissions after you click “Use page selection.” The extension does not continuously inspect webpages, retrieve full browsing history or access other tabs in the background.

3. Local processing, storage and sharing
Text cleaning happens on your device in the extension popup. Pomento TextClean does not upload your input text, cleaned output, selected webpage content or browsing activity to Pomento, the developer, an AI service or another server. The extension has no analytics service, advertising SDK, user account or tracking system.

Text remains in the popup’s temporary memory while it is open; the extension does not save text in persistent browser extension storage. Clicking “Clear all” removes the text from the popup, and closing the popup ends that editing session.

If you choose “Copy cleaned text,” the result goes to your device clipboard. If you choose to download it, the extension creates a plain-text file on your device. The operating system and browser control subsequent handling of clipboard content and downloaded files.

Pomento does not receive, sell or share the text you process through the extension.

4. Optional external contact links
The popup offers optional links to Pomento.in, Dr. Dinesh Kumar Jangra’s LinkedIn profile and an email contact option. These links open only when you choose to click them. They do not include or transmit your input or output text.

When you visit an external website or send an email, the receiving website, email service or your mail application may process the usual information associated with that action under its own privacy terms. You do not need to use these links to use the cleaner.

5. Retention and deletion
Pomento does not retain a server-side copy of your text because the extension does not transmit it. To remove text displayed in the current popup, click “Clear all” or close the popup. You can also uninstall the extension at any time. You are responsible for any files you have downloaded or text copied into other applications.

6. Chrome Web Store Limited Use
Any user data accessed by Pomento TextClean is used solely to provide its disclosed text-cleaning functionality. The extension does not sell user data, use it for personalized advertising, transfer it for unrelated purposes, or use it for creditworthiness or lending decisions. The developer cannot read text processed locally through the extension.

7. Changes to this policy
This policy describes version 1.0.0. If later versions change the way the extension handles data, this policy and the Chrome Web Store disclosures will be updated before the changed functionality is released.
